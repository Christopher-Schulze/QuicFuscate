# TODO-563: Lock the Post-Parity VisionOS Liquid Glass System

## Why

Certified parity is the safety baseline, not the final product direction. The requested final experience is a substantially more sophisticated, premium, product-specific VisionOS Liquid Glass system with excellent UX. That redesign needs an accepted concept contract, shared tokens, primitive ownership, motion grammar, accessibility fallbacks, and measurable performance budgets before either Dioxus surface changes.

## Acceptance

- Invoke and follow `frontend-cr-june26` for every action. Use its concept-first workflow, Image Gen where the concept needs original visual assets, component-integrity rules, quality gates, and rendered comparison loop.
- Start only from the certified TODO-552 Dioxus behavior and state inventory. Preserve every supported workflow, negative path, platform capability, and operational control unless an explicitly recorded redesign decision improves its presentation without reducing functionality.
- Keep `apps/svelte-admin/`, `apps/svelte-desktop/`, `apps/tauri/`, `packages/ui/`, `packages/theme/`, and `assets/web-admin/` byte-identical. All implementation remains under `Dioxus-UI/`.
- Verify current stable Dioxus capabilities and current primary-source platform guidance for glass materials, contrast, motion, depth, accessibility, and reduced transparency before selecting APIs or material behavior.
- Define a product-specific design thesis around private-network control, connection state, routing, protection, and operational evidence. Generic glass cards, decorative blur, gradient blobs, fake dashboards, and platform imitation without product meaning fail.
- Produce and score concepts for the web-admin and desktop primary viewports plus their important dense, modal, error, and mobile or constrained-window states. Every critical concept dimension must score at least 4/5.
- Establish one shared token and primitive system for material layers, color, typography, spacing, geometry, elevation, borders, icons, data density, focus, status semantics, motion, and reduced-transparency behavior.
- Name primitive owners and explicit variants before implementation. Wrapper-only components, duplicated component families, arbitrary-value drift, contradictory utilities, and more than two or three local overrides on repeated controls are forbidden.
- Define a coherent motion system with duration bands, easing, state transitions, reduced motion, no hover or focus growth, no layout-shifting microinteraction, and explicit CPU/GPU budgets.
- Prove readable contrast and clear state communication with transparency, reduced transparency, increased contrast, reduced motion, keyboard navigation, zoom, text scaling, and assistive technology.
- Freeze the accepted concept set, copy authority, asset manifest, component contract, state matrix, responsive plan, fidelity ledger, and implementation budgets before TODO-564 or TODO-565 starts.

## Completion Gates

- Concept gate: all required web and desktop concepts, state views, responsive views, and the concept contract are complete, product-specific, implementable, and score at least 4/5 on every critical dimension.
- System gate: tokens, primitive owners, variants, material fallbacks, motion grammar, icon treatment, density rules, and accessibility behavior have one unambiguous Dioxus implementation owner.
- Feasibility gate: prototypes prove the selected material, blur, compositing, motion, responsive, and reduced-transparency behavior within the target performance budgets.
- Protection gate: protected-path manifests remain identical and no protected UI file appears in the diff.
- Truth gate: the accepted direction, exact references, selected APIs, budgets, commands, artifacts, and intentional deviations are recorded in the owning docs and task files.
